# Admin Panel

**Branding:** the panel is called **NeboTV** ("nebo" = sky). The name comes from `config('app.name')` (`APP_NAME`, default `NeboTV`) and is shown in every `<title>` and, as a wordmark via `<x-brand-name />` (a trailing "TV" gets the `--brand-from`/`--brand-to` gradient), in the sidebar, the mobile top bar and the sign-in page; it is a brand name, so it is not translated. The logo is the `nebotv` icon (a TV with a star) on the indigo gradient `.brand-mark`. Favicons and home-screen icons live in `public/` (`favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `site.webmanifest`) and are linked from `admin/partials/favicons`, included by the layout and the sign-in page. Full logo (icon + wordmark) for use outside the panel: `public/logo.svg` / `logo.png` (light backgrounds) and `public/logo-dark.svg` / `logo-dark.png` (dark backgrounds); the SVGs use system fonts, so use the PNGs where exact lettering matters.

**Layout:** `resources/views/layouts/admin.blade.php`. Sidebar navigation (see *Menu and tabs* below), an alerts bell in the header ([alerts.md](alerts.md)), flash messages and validation errors rendered once for every page (`admin/partials/flash`), a language menu with flags (English / Macedonian, see [localization.md](localization.md)) and a light / dark / system switch ([theme.md](theme.md)) above the account footer, and a slide-out menu below 860 px. Styling lives in `public/css/admin.css` (see [architecture.md](../architecture.md#ui-conventions)). The theme follows the OS setting unless the admin picks light or dark.

**Menu and tabs** (`App\Support\AdminNav`, the only place to add a page to the menu): the sidebar groups pages by what you are doing:
- *Overview*: Dashboard, Alerts, Watching now (`admin.live.index`).
- *Channels*: Channels, Bouquets, Categories, Sources (with the Receiver page), TV guide.
- *Users*: Users, Hide channels, Activity.
- *Money*: one link to Payments.
- *Reports*: Statistics.
- *Maintenance*: Backups, Audit log, Launch checklist.
- **Security** (your two-step sign-in) is the shield button next to *Sign out* under your name. Language, light/dark mode and the alerts bell are in the top-right of the header.
- **Hiding the menu:** the sidebar button at the top left of the header shrinks the menu to a strip of icons (pointing at or tabbing to an icon shows the page name, `.nav-tip`) and back. The choice is remembered in this browser (`localStorage` key `nav`). On a phone (≤ 860 px) the menu button opens the full menu over the page instead.
- **Header:** one frosted bar on every page (`.header-bar`). Once the page's large title scrolls under it, the title appears small in the middle of the bar.
- **Error pages** (`resources/views/errors/`: 401, 403, 404, 419, 429, 500, 503, sharing `errors/layout`): the NeboTV mark, the code, a short message and *Go back*; a signed-in admin also gets *Go to the dashboard*. The layout asks nothing of the database or session (wrapped in `rescue()`), so the 500 page works when the app is broken.

Pages that belong together share a tab bar above the page (`.tabs`, the current one `aria-current="page"`, scrolled into view on a narrow screen) instead of each having a menu link:
- *Channels*: All channels · Duplicates · Cleanup rules · Playlist options.
- *Activity*: Plays · Refused.
- *Money*: Payments · Sales · Statements · Credit history.

The menu link of a section stays highlighted on all its tabs. Resellers see Overview, Users and Money (Payments, Statements, Credit history), and sections without a link they can open are left out. The addresses of all pages are unchanged.

Shared behaviours (in the layout script):
- `<form data-confirm="…">` asks for confirmation before submitting (all deletes).
- `<button data-copy="#input">` copies an input's value.

## Dashboard (`admin.dashboard`)
- Stats: active subscribers / total, enabled channels / total, **watching now** (sessions active in the last 5 min), plays in 24 h.
- A red banner when background tasks stopped: the scheduler hasn't run for 5 minutes (or never), or queued jobs have waited 15 minutes ([alerts.md](alerts.md#background-tasks-healthservice)).
- A "Get started" hint while there are no sources.
- Recent activity (8 latest plays), **Watched channels** that are not working ([alerts.md](alerts.md)), source list with sync status, subscriptions expiring within 7 days.
- The Channels stat links to the channels the stream check switched off.

## Sources (`admin.sources.*`, `POST sources/{source}/sync`)
Three kinds: **From URL**, **Upload file**, **Satellite receiver** (relay address, shared secret with copy button and a generated suggestion, transcoding switch; see [receiver.md](receiver.md)). Receiver rows show *receiver online / offline / not checked yet*. The *Live TV only* and *Check streams* options are hidden for receivers.

List with type/host, status badge (Not synced / Queued / Syncing / Synced / Failed with the error), channel counts (linked to the filtered channel list), bouquet, last sync, and Sync / Edit / Delete. See [sources.md](sources.md).

## Channels (`admin.channels.*`, `POST channels/bulk`)
- 50 per page, sorted by name, with logo, upstream host, category, source, bouquet count and status.
- **Filters** (GET, kept in pagination links): search by name, category (or *Uncategorized*), source (or *Added manually*), bouquet, status (*Enabled*, *Disabled*, *Not working* = switched off by the stream check, *Alerts on*, *Playing a backup*, *Slow*, *Often not working* = under 90% of last week's checks, *Viewers had trouble*, *Backup copies*, *Not watched in 30 days*, *No TV guide*). Bulk action *List again (backup copies)*.
- Under the status badge: the quality label, "N% working", *Slow* and *Viewers had trouble* ([channel-health.md](channel-health.md)). **Playlist options** button: [playlist-options.md](playlist-options.md).
- **Check streams** button: queues a stream check of all channels, or of the filtered source ([alerts.md](alerts.md)). Channels switched off by it show a red *Not working* badge.
- **Bulk actions:** tick rows (or the header box for the whole page). Once a full page is selected, *Select all N matching* applies the action to every channel matching the current filters. Actions: enable, disable, add to bouquet, remove from bouquet, turn alerts on, turn alerts off, delete. Adding skips channels already in the bouquet.
- **Watch** (play button on each row) opens a player dialog to check a channel by eye. The stream goes through the panel (`GET channels/{channel}/preview`, `StreamPreviewService`), so provider streams without CORS headers or on plain http still play. HLS (`.m3u8`/`.m3u`) uses hls.js and MPEG-TS uses mpegts.js, both loaded from jsDelivr on first use. If the first one fails, the other is tried. When nothing plays, the dialog shows the panel's reason (provider error code, web page instead of a stream, no answer) or a generic "format browsers can not play" message. The dialog also shows the stream address with a copy button, for VLC. Live MPEG-TS is passed on for at most 30 minutes per open player (`StreamPreviewService::MAX_SECONDS`) and ties up one PHP worker while it plays.
- Create/edit form: name, stream URL, optional category and logo, enabled toggle (hidden `0` fallback), **Send me alerts**. Both have **Channel number** (empty = next free number / keep it). Editing also has **Guide ID** (with the automatic match and *On now*) and **Backup channel** (channels with the same name or guide id), and a **How well it works** card above the form: reliability over 7 days, speed, picture, viewers with trouble, and the working copies used as backups, best first. Editing an imported channel shows a notice that the next sync overwrites name/URL/logo/category but keeps enabled/disabled.

- **Find duplicates** (button on the Channels page): the same channel listed more than once, with the best copy suggested and the rest switched off, see [duplicate-channels.md](duplicate-channels.md).

Guide sources with status and counts, coverage stats (enabled channels with a guide / with upcoming programmes), **Match channels again**, and an add form. See [epg.md](epg.md).

## Alerts (`admin.alerts.index`, `POST alerts/read`)
All alerts, newest first, 50 per page, with **Mark all as read**. See [alerts.md](alerts.md).

## Categories (`admin.categories.*`, only index/store/update/destroy)
Table with channel and enabled counts, *View channels* (filtered list), rename and create in dialogs, delete (channels keep existing without a category).

## Bouquets (`admin.bouquets.*`)
- List with channel count (and enabled count when different), user count, and a link to the bouquet's channels.
- Create/edit: name plus **add channels by category** (filterable tiles with channel counts). Ticked categories' channels are added; nothing is removed. Individual channels are managed with Channels-page bulk actions.

## Resellers
A **reseller** (`users.role = reseller`) signs in to the panel but only sees and manages their own users (`users.owner_id`). Set up by an admin in the user form: *Role: Reseller*, *Most users* (empty = no limit), *Most streams per user* (empty = no limit) and the **Bouquets**, which for a reseller means the bouquets they may give to their users (the same pivot; it also gives them a playlist of their own to try things). The admin's *Users* form can also put a subscriber under a reseller (*Reseller*), and the user list shows *Reseller: name* and can be filtered with `?reseller=<id>`.

What a reseller gets: **Dashboard** (own users, expiring soon, watching now, recent plays; "You have N of M users"), **Users** (only their own, create/edit/enable-disable/delete/new playlist link/disconnect devices), **Hide channels** (see the channels of their bouquets and hide them for all their users, or for one user from the user's page: [hide-channels.md](hide-channels.md)), **Activity** (plays of their users), **Alerts** (alerts about their users, including *asks to renew*; read/unread stays with the admins, so no unread count and *Mark all as read* does nothing for them) and **Security** (own two-factor and sign-ins). Everything else (Sources, Channels, Categories, Bouquets, TV guide, Statistics) answers **403**.

Rules, enforced on the server whatever is posted (`UserController::rules()` / `applyRestrictions()`): a new user is always a subscriber with `owner_id` = the reseller, role/owner/limits can't be changed by a reseller, they can only hand out their own bouquets (bouquets the admin gave a user are kept when the reseller saves), `max_connections` can't exceed their cap, and they can't add a user past *Most users*. Every `{user}` in a panel URL is scoped (`User::scopeVisibleTo`, `Route::bind('user')`): someone else's user, an admin or another reseller is a **404**, for show, edit, update, delete, toggle, playlist link and device disconnect alike. `EnsureAdmin` / `EnsureStaff` run before the binding.

An admin can't demote themselves, can't change a reseller that still has users into something else, and can't delete one that still has users (the error says to move or delete them first); if the account is removed some other way the users stay, without an owner. A reseller who is switched off or expired loses panel access, but their users keep watching until their own expiry date: the subscription belongs to the user. A bouquet taken from a reseller later stays on users who already have it. A reseller sees only the bouquets they hold on a user's page. Usernames are unique across the whole system, so a reseller can find out that a name is taken. Rolling the reseller migration back turns resellers into disabled subscribers. **Credits** limit how many subscriptions a reseller can give: see below. A reseller without a credit balance is not limited and can set any expiry date.

### Credits
**1 credit = 1 month** of subscription for one user (`App\Support\Credits`). Only resellers have them (`users.credits`; empty = not limited by credits, which is how every reseller starts and what existing ones stay). An admin gives credits (or takes them back, never below 0) on the reseller's page or sets *Starting credits* when creating the reseller; giving credits to an unlimited reseller switches the limit on, *Make unlimited* switches it off again.
- **What costs credits:** a reseller creating a user, or moving a user's end date later. The price is the whole calendar months added, rounded up, counted from the day the user's current subscription runs out (or from today when it already ended or has no end date) to the new end date. Keeping or shortening an end date, enabling/disabling, and every other edit are free. One extra day is not charged (the reseller's date can differ from the server's by a day: time zones), but any longer subscription costs at least 1 credit, so a subscription can't be extended a day at a time for free. There are no refunds (an admin can give credits back).
- **Rules for a reseller with credits:** an end date is required (no "Never"); the price is shown while typing (JavaScript copy of the same rule, the server decides); if they can't pay, nothing is created or changed and the form says *Not enough credits: this needs N, you have M*. A user and its credits are saved together, under a lock on the reseller's row, so parallel requests can't spend the same credits twice. The success message says *N credits used, M left*. Admins editing users never spend credits. A reseller can't change any balance (not in any form).
- **History:** every change is a row in `credit_transactions` (who did it, what, the user it was spent on, the note, the balance afterwards), never edited or deleted (when a user is deleted the row stays without the name). **Credits** in the sidebar: a reseller sees own balance and history, an admin everyone's or one reseller's (`?reseller=`). The reseller dashboard shows the balance; the user list shows each reseller's.

## Users (`admin.users.*`, `POST users/{user}/toggle`, `POST users/{user}/playlist-token`)
- List: search by username, status filter (active / expired / disabled), bouquets, expiry, max streams, and quick actions (playlist, enable/disable, edit, delete; you can't delete yourself).
- **Export / Import** (buttons on the list; admins and resellers, a reseller's own users): *Export* downloads the users the list filters match as CSV (username, role, status, email, expiration_date, max_connections, bouquets joined with `|`, trial, created_at, plus the reseller for admins; no passwords unless `?passwords=1`, which is audit-logged). Cells that start with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` get a quote in front so a spreadsheet can't run them. *Import* (`users/import`) creates up to 2,000 users from a CSV (comma or semicolon, header row; columns `username`, `password`, `expiration_date`, `max_connections`, `bouquets`, `email`, `status`; an example file to download). Each row is checked like the user form; a bad row is listed (row number and reason) and skipped, the others are created. Empty passwords are made up and shown once in a copyable list. A reseller's users are theirs, with only bouquets they hold, within *Most streams per user*, *Most users* and credits (an end date is required with credits; creation stops with an error per row when credits run out). *Only check the file* (ticked by default) reports everything and creates nothing.
- **Bulk actions** (checkboxes in the list; *Select all N matching* takes everyone the search / status / reseller filters match): *Enable*, *Disable*, *Renew* (1 / 3 / 6 / 12 months), *Add a bouquet*, *Remove a bouquet*, *Delete* (`POST users/bulk`, `UserBulkService`). Only **subscribers** are touched (never admins, resellers or yourself). A reseller works with their own users and the bouquets they hold; *Renew* costs them 1 credit per month per user, **all or nothing** (if the credits don't cover everyone, nothing changes), users who never expire are skipped, a trial becomes a normal user. Set-based queries, so it works on hundreds of users; written to the audit log.
- Form: username, password (text field, min 6, blank on edit = keep), role (subscriber / admin), status, expiry date with +1 month / +3 months / +1 year / Never shortcuts, simultaneous streams, bouquets (tiles with channel counts). Unticking all bouquets clears them.
- **New trial** (`admin.users.trial`, button on the list; admins and resellers): a short test account with a random username (`trial12345`) and password, one device, ending in 24 hours / 3 days / 7 days, on the chosen bouquets (a reseller: their own). It costs no credits, but counts against a reseller's user limit and a reseller can make `TRIALS_PER_DAY` (5) per 24 hours; admins are not limited. A *Trial* badge shows in the list and on the user page and Xtream apps see `is_trial`; moving the end date later (user form) turns it into a normal user. Trials are not deleted automatically.
- **User page** (`admin.users.show`; create and update redirect here):
  - **Playlist link** `/playlist/{token}.m3u` with copy button and *New link* (regenerates the token; the old link stops working).
  - Warnings when the panel is opened on localhost (link unreachable from a TV), when the account is disabled/expired, or when the playlist would be empty.
  - TV setup steps (TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, OTT Navigator, VLC).
  - **Log in with username and password** (Xtream Codes): server URL (`scheme://host:port` of the current request) and username with copy buttons, the **password**, hidden until you press *Show current password*, with a *Copy* button (from the encrypted copy kept since passwords started being stored; for users without one it says so and links to *Change password*), host and port separately, and setup steps. See [playlist-and-streaming.md](playlist-and-streaming.md#3-xtream-codes-api-login-with-server--username--password).
  - **Message for the user:** a ready-to-send text (server URL, username, password when stored, playlist link, the user's page, end date) with a *Copy message* button, to paste into WhatsApp / Viber / email. Written in the user's own language (`users.locale`) when set, else the panel's. Admins and resellers (own users) get it.
  - **Page for this user:** the link `/me/{playlist token}` with a copy button, to send to the subscriber (see below).
  - Subscription details (expiry, active/max streams, channel count, bouquets) and the 10 latest plays. "Active now" counts only devices that played within the last 5 minutes.
  - **Renew** (in the Subscription box, subscribers with an end date): pick 1 / 3 / 6 / 12 months and press *Renew*. The end date moves later by that many calendar months from the current end date, or from now when it already ended (`POST users/{user}/renew`, `UserController::renew`). An **admin renews for free**; a **reseller** with credits pays 1 credit per month (`Credits::months`, ledger reason *Subscription extended*), a reseller without credits pays nothing, and not enough credits refuses the renewal and changes nothing. A trial becomes a normal user, unread alerts about the user's subscription (expiring, expired, asks to renew) are marked read, and the renewal is written to the audit log. The *Alerts* page has a **Renew 1 month** button on those alerts.
  - **Devices watching now:** one row per device (channel, IP, player, since) with **Disconnect** and, for more than one device, **Disconnect all**. Disconnecting deletes the device's session (freeing its slot) and keeps that device (IP + player) from starting a stream for `StreamService::DEVICE_BLOCK_MINUTES` (15). The block is kept in the cache. A player that streams straight from the provider (redirect) keeps its current stream until it reconnects, because the panel is no longer in the path; through *Hide provider address* HLS parts are refused at once and a running MPEG-TS stream stops at the next 30-second tick.

**QR codes** (`App\Support\QrCode`, inline SVG made on the server, dark on white in both themes): the user page has *Show as QR codes* with the user's own page and the playlist link, to scan with a phone instead of typing; the user's own page has the playlist link as a QR code.

## Subscriber page (`/me/{playlist token}`)
No sign-in: the subscriber opens their personal link (the same secret as the playlist link, shown on the user page as *Page for this user*; a new playlist link retires it). `SelfServiceController`, routes in `routes/player.php` (no session, no cookies, no CSRF), limit 30 requests a minute per link + IP (`me` limiter). Shows status (active / expired / disabled) and the expiry date, the playlist link with a copy button, how to set it up, the Xtream server and username (**never the password**: it is stored hashed; the page says it is the one they were given), the channel count and bouquet names, and "streams in use: N of max". **Ask to renew** appears when the subscription ends within 30 days or has ended (not for disabled accounts or ones without an end date): it creates a `renewal_requested` alert for the admins (panel, link to the user, *Subscriptions* group in the email digest), at most one per user per 24 hours; the page then confirms ("sent" / "already asked today"). Language: `?lang=en|mk`, otherwise the browser's language, nothing stored. Unknown or retired links, and admin accounts, get a plain 404. Marked `noindex`, `no-referrer`.

## Statement (`admin.statements.index`)
A reseller's month in numbers (reseller: their own; admin: pick the reseller and the month, also linked from the Sales table): **opening and closing balance** (shown as a ledger: opening, + given, − taken back, − used for new subscriptions and renewals, closing; opening is 0 for a reseller's first month), credits **given**, **taken back** and **used for new subscriptions / renewals**, the number of **new subscriptions** and **renewals**, users **added**, subscriptions **ended** and **active now**, and the month's credit history line by line (up to 1,000). *Download CSV* (spreadsheet-safe) and *Print* (the menu and buttons are left out of the print). A reseller without credits shows "not limited by credits". Months go back to the first credit entry (up to 36). `ResellerStatementService`, from `credit_transactions` and `users`.

## Refused plays (Activity → Refused, `admin.logs.refused`)
Every play the panel refused, with the **reason and what to do about it**: account disabled, subscription ended, device disconnected, channel switched off, channel hidden for this user, channel not in their bouquets, too many devices at once (`StreamService::denialReason`, stored in `denied_plays` by `StreamService::authorize` for `/play` and Xtream `/live`). The same user, channel and reason is recorded once a minute so a retrying player doesn't fill the list. Filters: reason and user. Kept `LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` (90). The user page shows the latest five. A reseller sees their own users. The app still answers the same 403 text: the reason is only for you.

## Backups (`admin.backups.index`, admins only)
The backups on every disk (the main one and the off-site copy), the time of the last one and the last passed check, and **Check the newest backup** (`POST backups/verify`): the newest file on each disk must exist, not be empty, read to the end as gzip, hold a table for every table the database has now (missing ones are named) and the users' rows, and every disk must have the same newest file. A pass ticks *A restore has been tested once* on the launch checklist. A warning shows when there is no off-site copy. See [deployment.md](../deployment.md#6-backups).

## Housekeeping of subscribers
`iptv:cleanup-users` runs daily at 05:00 (`UserCleanupService`): a **trial** is deleted `TRIAL_KEEP_DAYS` (7) days after it ended, and an active **subscriber** whose subscription ended more than `EXPIRED_DISABLE_DAYS` (60) days ago is switched off (`0` = never for either). Admins and resellers are never touched. Both are written to the audit log as "system" with the number and the first names. **Renewing** (the Renew button, the alert button, bulk renew) turns a switched-off user back on.

## Watching now (`admin.live.index`)
Who is watching right now: the devices that played something in the last 5 minutes (`ActiveSession::active()`), newest first, up to 500: user, channel, IP, player and since when, with **Disconnect** (same as on the user page: the device can't start a stream for 10 minutes). Cards: devices watching (and how many users) and the most watched channel now. Refreshes itself every 20 seconds (a checkbox, remembered in the browser). An admin sees everyone, a reseller their own users.

## Sharing alerts and addresses
`iptv:detect-sharing` runs hourly (`SharingDetectionService`): a subscriber who started streams from at least `SHARING_IPS` (3) different IP addresses within `SHARING_HOURS` (24) gets a **sharing alert** (once a day per user, `0` turns it off). The alert is on the Alerts page for the admin and for the reseller whose user it is, and in the email digest (under *Subscriptions*). The user page has **Where this user watches from**: the addresses of the last 7 days with their plays and last use, and a badge when the user is at or over the limit. Phones change addresses, which is why the default is a high 3 in a day; look at the list before you act.

## Sales (`admin.sales.index`, admins only)
What each reseller sold. Period menu: last 30 / 90 / 365 days (`?days=`). Cards: **months sold** (credits spent on new and extended subscriptions, 1 credit = 1 month; credits given as the note), **new users**, users **expiring in 30 days** and users that **expired in the last 30 days** (not renewed). A column chart shows months sold for each of the last 12 months, and a table has one row per reseller: months sold, credits given, balance with *about N days left* (balance divided by the daily pace of the last 30 days; yellow under 14 days, shown for resellers with a credit balance who sold something), users (active / all), new, expiring and expired, plus a row for the admins' own users. Read from the credit history and the users table with grouped queries (`SalesReportService`); a reseller without a credit balance shows users but no sales, since nothing is counted for them. Admins only.

## Audit log (`admin.audit.index`, admins only)
Who changed what in the panel, newest first, 50 per page, with filters for the person, the kind of change and a search on the name it was about. Recorded (by `AuditService`, right after the change succeeded; a failure to write never blocks the change): user created / changed (what changed, e.g. `Status: active → disabled`, *Password changed* without the password, bouquets added / removed, credits used) / deleted / enabled / disabled, trial created, new playlist link, devices disconnected, credits given / taken back / made unlimited, channels hidden / shown (for one user, or for all users of an owner), and deleting a source, a bouquet or channels. A change that changes nothing is not recorded. Names are stored as text, so entries stay readable after the person or user is deleted (the user is linked only while they exist). Kept 365 days (`AuditLog::KEEP_DAYS`, pruned with `model:prune`). Resellers have no access (403): this is the admins' overview of what resellers do. Plays, sign-ins and credit spending have their own pages (Activity, Security, Credits).

## Launch checklist (`admin.launch-checklist.index`, admins only)
What to have in place before real viewers and resellers use the panel, in four groups: **Security** (two-factor on for the signed-in admin, no admin with the sample password `admin123`, `APP_ENV=production` with `APP_DEBUG=false`, https address with a secure session cookie, shared secrets replaced), **Backups** (a backup within `BackupService::STALE_HOURS`, an off-site copy disk set, `APP_KEY` saved elsewhere, a restore tested), **Running in the background** (scheduler heartbeat and queue from `HealthService`, a real mailer plus `ALERT_MAIL_TO`, an outside uptime monitor, Sentry as optional) and **Ready for viewers and resellers** (a source with channels, a bouquet with channels, a first user, a channel played on a real TV app, a reseller tried end to end). The header shows *N of M done* (optional items are not counted).

`LaunchChecklistService` checks the automatic items live on every visit (nothing is stored for them). Items that can't be seen from the server (a password manager, an outside monitor) are manual: *Mark as done* / *Undo* stores the item in `checklist_ticks` (item key unique, who ticked it). Only the manual keys are accepted; anything else answers 404. A reseller gets 403 and the menu entry is hidden.

## Payments (`admin.payments.index`, admins and resellers)
Prices, sales with *Mark paid*, what resellers owe for credits: see [payments.md](payments.md).

## Statistics (`admin.statistics.index`)
What users watch, from the play history (`stream_logs`, kept `LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` = 90 days). Period menu: last 7 / 30 / 90 days (`?days=`, anything else = 30). Shows total **plays** (one stream start; switching channel counts again) with the daily average, **viewers** (users who watched), **channels watched**, plays per day and per hour of the day as column charts (hover for the number; times are in `APP_TIMEZONE`, UTC by default), the 10 most played channels (plays, distinct viewers) and the 10 most active users (plays, distinct channels). Also **most streams at once** (when, and per day as a chart), **per source** (plays, viewers, most at once; receiver marked) and how many listed channels **nobody played** (links to the Channels filter *Not watched in 30 days*). Streams at once come from `iptv:sample-viewing` every 5 minutes (`viewing_samples`: active sessions per source, kept like the play history), so they start counting after the deploy. `StatisticsService` uses a fixed number of grouped queries over the `started_at` index (never loads rows) and caches the result for 5 minutes (plain arrays and strings only: the database cache can't hold objects), so new plays can take up to 5 minutes to appear. Plays of a deleted channel or user are deleted with them.

## Activity (`admin.logs.index`)
Stream starts, newest first, 50 per page, filterable by user (`?user=`), with IP and player user agent.
